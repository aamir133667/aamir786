My learning method

I try to learn useful words and sentences that I can use in real conversations, instead of only memorizing.

  1. Learn I pick up new words, phrases and grammar.
  2. Listen I listen to how the language sounds when people use it.
  3. Speak I say the words and sentences out loud.
  4. Read I read to see the language in context.
  5. Write I write my own sentences.
  6. Practice I repeat and use what I learned until it becomes natural.

My goal

I want to be comfortable communicating in multiple languages while I keep improving my vocabulary, pronunciation, grammar and confidence.

Languages connect people.
Learning languages connects me to the world.
